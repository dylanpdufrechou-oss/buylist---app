Guidelines

CIB Only - Strict Standards

We currently accept 9/10 Complete In Box (CIB) copies only.

No loose, incomplete, or reproduction copies.

📦 CIB Requirements

Each game must include:

  • Original case
  • Original cover artwork insert
  • Original manual (if originally included)
  • All original inserts
  • Correct disc or cartridge

⭐ Condition Standard

Games must be clean and fully intact. No major scratches, cracks, water damage, heavy wear, or artwork damage. Light shelf wear is acceptable.

⚠ Additional Requirements

  • Wii U and Nintendo 3DS titles must include the original white insert.
  • No GameStop stickers on the artwork insert (stickers on outer case are acceptable).
  • Reprinted or reproduction artwork is not accepted.

Shipping

Most orders are processed within 48 hours of arrival.

  • Use bubble wrap to protect cases.
  • Do not ship loose discs.
  • Include your printed packing slip in every box.
  • Tracking is required for all shipments.
  • Secure contents tightly to prevent case cracking.
